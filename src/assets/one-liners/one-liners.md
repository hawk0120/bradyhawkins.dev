## One liners for myself

Art is a the glue that keeps the world together. I'm not hardwired to appreciate it, its through the constant growth as an individual that as given me an appreciate for the Arts. I excelled at Science and the more tangible realities of life. One peice that I've grown to love is a good turn of phrase. Its the glue of a strong argument. The humour in our lives. The love that binds us. In a sense words are what make the world go round.

I've collected a list slowly over the past few years, its grown quite useful when I'm in a hard place, giving advice, or getting advice.
Here's some that I go back to:

Real boats, rock.
Keep it simple.
Nuisances left unattended grow into real problems.
Nothing is as good or as bad as it seems.
You can do anything, but not everything
Emotional hurt is the price you pay for independence.
Life is war, and a journey far from home.
